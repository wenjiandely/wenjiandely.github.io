I worked on a PYNQ-Z2 vision platform, implementing Sobel edge detection, Gaussian filtering, image resizing, and lookup-table mapping in Vivado HLS. The platform connects webcam capture to FPGA processing and output.

Our team won second prize in the FPGA Innovation Design track at the finals of the 2025 National Undergraduate Embedded Chip and System Design Competition.
