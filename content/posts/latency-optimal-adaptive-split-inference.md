## Overview

The end device runs the initial CNN layers, encrypts their output, and keeps the secret key locally. Edge and cloud servers process the remaining layers on ciphertexts. A planner chooses two partition points by accounting for local computation, encryption, data transfer, and encrypted execution.

On CIFAR-10 and PathMNIST, convolution-level partitioning achieves speedups of about 12.9× over cloud-only FHE and 3.9× over block-level partitioning. These figures use amortized end-to-end latency; classification accuracy matches the corresponding plaintext models.
